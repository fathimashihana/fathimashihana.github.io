## Overview

Throughout my academic and research career, I have actively mentored undergraduate and postgraduate students, research assistants, and laboratory personnel across toxicology, biomarker discovery, molecular biology, and translational medicine. My supervision experience spans honours projects, MPhil and PhD mentoring, summer research internships, and laboratory staff training. Many trainees have progressed to successful careers as Scientific Officers, Research Assistants, and postgraduate researchers in academia and healthcare institutions.

## Research Supervision Experience

### 2022

**Supervisor – Centenary Institute Summer Research Project**

* Supervised undergraduate summer research students undertaking laboratory-based biomedical research projects.
* Provided guidance on experimental design, laboratory techniques, data analysis, and scientific presentation.

### 2021

**Supervisor – Centenary Institute Summer Research Project**

* Mentored students conducting translational biomedical research projects.

**Research Assistant Training**

* Trained research assistants in laboratory procedures, molecular biology techniques, data management, and research documentation.

### 2013–2016

**Research Associate and Laboratory Team Leader**

## Outcomes and Impact

* Supervised students from diverse scientific disciplines including toxicology, biomedical sciences, molecular biology, and clinical research.
* Undergraduate students under my supervision contributed to **two peer-reviewed research publications**.
* Postgraduate students whom I mentored contributed to **four peer-reviewed research publications**.
* All seven laboratory analysts trained under my supervision were subsequently appointed as **Laboratory Scientific Officers in Sri Lankan hospitals**.
* One MPhil student progressed to doctoral studies and currently works as a **Research Assistant at the Postgraduate Institute of Agriculture, Sri Lanka**.
* Contributed to capacity building in toxicology, biomarker discovery, and translational research through mentorship, technical training, and scientific leadership.
