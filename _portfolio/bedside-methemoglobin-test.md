## Overview

Developed and validated a simple bedside colour chart for measuring methemoglobin levels in poisoned patients.

## Key Achievements

* Reduced mortality from propanil poisoning
* Included in Sri Lankan national guidelines
* Used in hospitals across Sri Lanka
* Referenced internationally

## Publications

* Ann Emerg Med. 2010
* Bull World Health Organ. 2016
* Clin Toxicol. 2016

## Impact

The bedside test improved antidote administration and reduced case fatality rates.
