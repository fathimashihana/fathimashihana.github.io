## Overview

Conducted translational research on snakebite envenoming to identify molecular biomarkers, understand disease mechanisms, and improve diagnostic approaches. The work explored venom-induced inflammation, kidney injury, coagulation abnormalities, and the potential role of microRNAs in precision diagnostics for snakebite victims.

## Key Achievements

- Investigated venom-induced acute kidney injury
- Evaluated bedside coagulation tests
- Developed microRNA biomarker concepts
- Established international collaborations

## Relevant Publications

- Thrombosis and Haemostasis. 2017
- Toxins. 2026
- Toxins (AKI Review)

## Impact

Improved understanding of snakebite pathophysiology and future diagnostic strategies.
