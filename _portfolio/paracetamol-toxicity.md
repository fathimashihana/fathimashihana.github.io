## Overview

Investigated molecular mechanisms and biomarkers associated with paracetamol overdose through the Australian Toxicology Monitoring Study. Research focused on liver injury, gastrointestinal toxicity, and treatment responses, contributing to improved understanding of overdose pathophysiology and patient outcomes.

## Key Achievements

- Conducted ATOM studies
- Biomarker discovery for GI injury
- Investigated treatment responses
- Clinical translational research

## Relevant Publications

- Gastroenterology & Hepatology. 2024
- Australian Toxicology Monitoring Study publications

## Impact

Enhanced understanding of paracetamol-induced organ injury and recovery mechanisms.
