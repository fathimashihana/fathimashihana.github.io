## Overview

Led research investigating novel biomarkers for early detection of acute kidney injury caused by poisoning and envenoming. The project focused on identifying sensitive molecular indicators that detect kidney damage before conventional clinical markers, improving diagnosis, prognosis, and patient management in toxicology settings.

## Key Achievements

- Established and managed the SACTRC Renal Biomarker Program.
- Led biomarker discovery studies in pesticide poisoning and envenomation.
- Managed international sample processing and biomarker assays.
- Trained researchers and laboratory analysts in renal biomarker techniques.

## Publications

- Scientific Reports. 2021
- Pharmacology Research & Perspectives. 2021
- Clinical Toxicology. 2025

## Impact

Established some of the first human evidence supporting microRNA biomarkers for toxic AKI and improved understanding of nephrotoxicity following poisoning and envenoming.
