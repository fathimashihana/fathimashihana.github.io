## Overview

Developed zebrafish models of alcohol-associated liver disease and non-alcoholic fatty liver disease to investigate disease progression and molecular pathways. The project combined imaging, transcriptomics, and biomarker discovery approaches to identify mechanisms underlying liver injury and potential therapeutic targets.

## Key Achievements

- Established zebrafish disease models
- Confocal imaging and phenotypic analyses
- Transcriptomic investigations
- Biomarker discovery studies

## Relevant Publications

- Biology. 2023
- Liver disease biomarker studies

## Impact

Provided new insights into liver disease mechanisms and potential therapeutic targets.
