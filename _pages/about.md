# Dr. Fathima Shihana

Welcome to my academic portfolio website.

I am a Research Scientist with expertise in Molecular Biology, Pharmacology, Toxicology, Clinical Genomics, and Data Analytics. My research focuses on translational medicine, biomarker discovery, zebrafish disease models, and advanced molecular techniques for understanding human disease and therapeutic interventions.

I have extensive experience in RT-qPCR, CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, Flow Cytometry, GC-MS/MS, ELISA, Next-Generation Sequencing, and bioinformatics analysis. Throughout my academic and professional career, I have contributed to multidisciplinary research projects, scientific publications, conference presentations, and translational health research initiatives.

My interests include molecular toxicology, pharmacogenomics, clinical genomics, biomarker development, precision medicine, and the application of data analytics and artificial intelligence in biomedical research.

I welcome opportunities for research collaboration, academic partnerships, and industry engagement.
