# Research Impact

My research has focused on translational clinical toxicology, biomarker discovery, molecular diagnostics, and experimental disease modelling. Through multidisciplinary collaborations across Australia and Sri Lanka, my work has contributed to improving patient outcomes in poisoning, snakebite envenoming, acute kidney injury, liver disease, and public health. A key strength of my research has been translating laboratory discoveries into practical clinical applications, particularly in resource-limited healthcare settings.

## Research Impact Highlights

- Developed a bedside methemoglobin diagnostic test adopted in Sri Lankan hospitals and incorporated into national poisoning management guidelines.
- Contributed to reducing propanil poisoning mortality from 9.5% to 3.1%.
- Published more than 28 peer-reviewed scientific publications.
- Delivered over 29 presentations at national and international scientific conferences.
- Advanced biomarker discovery research in toxic acute kidney injury, snakebite envenoming, and poisoning.
- Established and applied zebrafish models of Alcohol-Associated Liver Disease (ALD) and Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD).
- Generated novel evidence supporting the use of circulating and urinary microRNAs as diagnostic biomarkers.
- Trained and mentored honours students, MPhil students, research assistants, and laboratory scientists.
- Established international collaborations across Australia, Sri Lanka, Brazil, the United Kingdom, and Denmark.

## Bedside Diagnostic Innovation

One of my most significant research contributions was the development and validation of a low-cost bedside colour chart for the diagnosis of methemoglobinemia in patients with propanil poisoning. Methemoglobinemia is a life-threatening condition that requires rapid diagnosis and timely antidote administration. In many hospitals, particularly in low-resource settings, laboratory testing is unavailable or delayed.

The bedside colour chart enabled healthcare professionals to estimate methemoglobin concentrations quickly and accurately, facilitating earlier treatment decisions and more effective antidote administration.

### Impact

- Reduced propanil poisoning mortality from 9.5% to 3.1%.
- Incorporated into Sri Lankan national poisoning management guidelines.
- Implemented in hospitals throughout Sri Lanka.
- Referenced in Australian toxicology guidance.
- Demonstrated successful translation of research into clinical practice.

## Biomarker Discovery for Toxic Acute Kidney Injury

My PhD and subsequent research focused on identifying novel biomarkers for early detection of acute kidney injury (AKI) resulting from poisoning, pesticide exposure, and snakebite envenoming. Conventional biomarkers such as serum creatinine often increase only after significant kidney damage has occurred.

I investigated molecular biomarkers, including urinary and circulating microRNAs, cystatin-C, KIM-1, NGAL, and other renal injury markers, to improve early diagnosis and risk stratification.

### Impact

- Generated some of the first human evidence supporting microRNAs as biomarkers for toxic AKI.
- Improved understanding of nephrotoxicity mechanisms following poisoning and envenoming.
- Supported development of non-invasive diagnostic approaches.
- Contributed to multiple high-impact publications in clinical toxicology and nephrology.

## MicroRNA Biomarker Research

My research has contributed substantially to the emerging field of microRNA-based diagnostics in toxicology. I investigated circulating and urinary microRNAs in acute kidney injury, pesticide poisoning, oxalic acid poisoning, snakebite envenoming, and paracetamol toxicity.

Using high-throughput OpenArray RT-qPCR platforms and advanced bioinformatics analyses, I identified novel molecular signatures associated with toxic injury and treatment response.

### Impact

- Advanced understanding of microRNA biology in toxicological injury.
- Established molecular biomarker discovery pipelines.
- Supported translational research toward precision toxicology.
- Generated novel diagnostic candidates for future clinical validation.

## Snakebite Envenoming Research

Snakebite envenoming is a neglected tropical disease affecting millions worldwide. My work investigated molecular mechanisms, kidney injury biomarkers, coagulation abnormalities, and treatment monitoring in snakebite victims.

Research included studies on Russell’s viper and Bothrops envenoming and explored how microRNAs and other biomarkers may improve diagnosis and prognosis.

### Impact

- Improved understanding of venom-induced acute kidney injury.
- Identified potential biomarkers associated with snakebite severity.
- Supported international collaborations in Australia and Brazil.
- Contributed to future diagnostic and therapeutic research directions.

## Paracetamol (Acetaminophen) Toxicity Research

As part of the Australian Toxicology Monitoring (ATOM) Study, I contributed to investigations examining biomarkers of liver injury, gastrointestinal toxicity, and treatment response in patients presenting with paracetamol overdose.

Research focused on identifying molecular changes occurring during antidote therapy and improving understanding of toxicity mechanisms.

### Impact

- Supported biomarker discovery for paracetamol-induced injury.
- Improved understanding of treatment responses during antidote administration.
- Contributed to translational clinical toxicology research.
- Strengthened collaborations among Australian toxicology centres.

## Alcohol-Associated Liver Disease and NAFLD Research

At the Centenary Institute and the University of Sydney, I developed and applied zebrafish models of Alcohol-Associated Liver Disease (ALD) and Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD) to investigate disease progression, molecular mechanisms, and potential therapeutic targets.

Using transcriptomic analyses, confocal microscopy, molecular biology techniques, and phenotypic assessments, I studied pathways involved in inflammation, lipid accumulation, oxidative stress, and liver injury.

### Impact

- Established experimental zebrafish models of ALD and NAFLD.
- Generated novel insights into molecular mechanisms driving liver disease progression.
- Produced peer-reviewed publications in liver disease research.
- Supported grant applications and future therapeutic investigations.
- Demonstrated the value of zebrafish as a translational model for chronic liver disease.
